# FAQ

Common questions about the trade-with-moot experiment.

## Is this financial advice?

**No.** This is research entertainment about an AI experiment. Watching the experiment ≠ what you should do with your money. Every post carries the disclaimer; the disclaimer is the operating principle.

## Are you a registered investment adviser?

**No.** The operator does not give personalized investment advice, does not run a managed account or fund, does not sell trade signals as a subscription product. Under the *Lowe v. SEC* publisher exemption and FINRA Rule 2210 framing, public after-the-fact commentary on an AI experiment is publisher territory, not adviser territory.

## Why anonymous?

To force the credibility focus onto the work, not the operator. Anonymous research has a long history (Pseudonymous quants on Twitter, Lil Miquela in marketing, etc.). The Day-1,095 retrospective will include optional identity reveal — by then the work will speak for itself or it won't.

## How can I trust the trades are real?

The Alpaca portfolio share link is pinned to every profile. You can verify cumulative P&L, drawdown, position count, and trade history any time. If a claim in this repository disagrees with the Alpaca link, **trust the Alpaca link** and file an issue.

## Can I see the bot's source code?

**No.** The bot is closed-source. The architecture is documented here; the parameters are not. The point of the experiment is the methodology demonstrated on a public account, not the recipe.

## Will the experiment make money?

**Unknown.** That's literally the experiment. If we knew the answer, we wouldn't need to run it for 3 years.

## What happens at Day 1,095?

A full retrospective: cumulative P&L, methodology evolution, what worked, what didn't, what's next. Optional identity reveal at operator's discretion. Whatever the outcome, the retrospective gets published. A bad outcome is not a reason to bury the experiment — that would defeat its purpose.

## How can I support this?

Cost-recovery donations only, via Patreon, Buy Me a Coffee, or GitHub Sponsors. Capped at 1.5× monthly compute cost; surplus goes to AI safety / open-source AI research nonprofits (METR, MIRI, Apollo Research, ARC Evals, EleutherAI, LAION, HF Open-Source Fund). No commercial monetization, ever.

## Why no merch?

Same reason as no course, no sponsorships, no paid signals: the moment we sell anything, we have a financial interest in the experiment looking good. The whole point is that the experiment's outcome — good, bad, or neutral — should be honestly published. No commercial layer.

## How do I contact you?

Via the issues tab on this repo. The operator's identity is private. Genuine methodology questions and corrections will get responses; harassment will be locked.
