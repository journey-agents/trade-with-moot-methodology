# Meta

Operational and meta-documentation about the experiment itself.

## Contents (planned)

- `commitment.md` — the public 1,095-day commitment text and signature
- `governance.md` — how decisions about methodology changes are made
- `transparency-fund.md` — cost-recovery donation accounting
- `revisions.md` — log of all methodology changes during the experiment
- `incidents.md` — operational incidents (outages, data quality issues, errors)

## Why this matters

A 3-year experiment needs operational meta-records, not just methodology docs. Future readers (and future operators) need to know:
- What changed mid-experiment and why
- Where operational discipline broke down (transparent record)
- What the actual cost structure was

This directory is the place for all of that.
